# Hat Concept

A web app that acts as if you put pieces of paper in a hat and randomly selected from it. It allows you to type your pieces of "paper" that get thrown in the hat. Then you hit "Pick" and it randomly grabs one of those "papers". 

Hat also allows you to save different "Hats". For example, maybe you have 5 restaurants you like to go to and you have these 5 restaurants saved under "My Favorite Restaurants Hat" and then when you click on that hat, it opens up with your saved restaurants and then randomly picks from there.
